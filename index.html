Questions

How do I listen on iPhone?

Press the play button. The station streams live in Safari, and keeps playing from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

Is it free?

Yes. GlowWave FM is free to listen to, with no sign-up and no App Store app required.

How do I add it to my Home Screen?

Tap the Share icon in Safari, then choose Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen, with no browser bars.

Why is there no sound?

Check the silent switch and volume on your iPhone, and the volume slider above. Browsers only start audio after you press play.

Where do the headlines come from?

The ticker links to live Reuters and Bellingcat coverage. Each headline opens the original article.
